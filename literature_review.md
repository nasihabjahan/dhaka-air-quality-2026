# Related Work
Due to the worsening of air quality caused by unplanned industrialization, continual studies are being performed on monitoring air pollution level. Presence of pollutants like SO2, NO, NO2, CO, O3, PM2.5, PM10 etc. in air cause detrimental health effects, and thus form the main particles of observation.  Due to the unprecedented success achieved by using machine learning in different fields, it is also being applied and studied in the detection of air pollution level. The machine learning models are trained with dataset containing the amount of different pollutants in air and the resultant AQI (Air Quality Index). The value of AQI is the unit that gives an estimation of the level of pollution in air. 

Many studies are being conducted based on Bangladesh to predict AQI using machine learning models. Rahman et el. (2025) conducted a study[1] by applying several machine learning classification models like LR, DT, RF, SVM, KNN, NB, RNN and CNN on dataset specifically based on industrial zones like Gazipur, Rajshahi, Narshingdi, Rangpur, Savar, Sylhet for years 2021 to 2023. The outcome was to predict the category that the AQI belonged to (Good, Satisfactory, Moderate, Poor, Very Poor, and Severe). Their comparative study on different models concluded DT as the best performing model with accuracy over 99% for all the cities, and RF being the second best model with accuracy above 90%.

Another study[2] conducted by Karim et el. (2025) shows the implementation of regression models of RF, KNN, DT and XGBoost on Kaggle dataset that includes air pollutants' level in Gazipur and Narayanganj in Dhaka, TV Station and Agrabad in Chittagong, and Sylhet, from year 2013 to 2022. The study found XGBoost achieving a high R2 value of 0.997.

Islam et el. (2024) and the Robotics Lab of Department of Software Engineeing, Daffodil International University, developed[3] an IoT framework and collected real-time air pollutants' data from Kuril Bishow Road, Uttara in Dhaka and Tongi in Gazipur, from years 2017 to 2022.  After preprocessing, they applied regression models LR, DT, RF and Gradient Boosting on the dataset. At the final stage, Gradient Boosting achieved MAE of 8.34 and RMSE of 17.86, which was of the lowest error and of the highest performance among all the models.

All these studies come with insightful findings, however most of them are limited to specific zones and cannot give insight on air pollution of overall Dhaka city. Also the models have been applied to dataset as past as 2023, and the latest 2026 dataset haven't been observed using machine learning algorithms. They measure the AQI based in available air pollutant measurement, but doesn't forecast the AQI for the following day. 

This study aims at predicting the value of AQI using different machine learning models like LR, RF, DT, SVM, and KNN on Kaggle's Dhaka Air Pollution dataset 2026, and observe the accuracy to find which model performs the best in predicting the following day's AQI.
